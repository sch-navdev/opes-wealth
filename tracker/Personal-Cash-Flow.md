## Steps 2 and 3 and the UAE gratuity calculator (2026-10-09)
- **Waterfall (step 2)** `lib/cash-flow-waterfall.ts` (+ server loader, 27 tests): Net Investable Cash = (income - planned liabilities) - daily expenses. Income = monthly equivalents of the income streams; liabilities from `monthly_payment` and linked loans; daily expenses = average monthly OUTFLOW of the imported `transactions` over the last N complete months (divided by the months that actually have data). Each exclusion is one toggle with count and amount: own-account transfers (same day, opposite amounts, two accounts; cross-currency never paired), credit-card settlement lines, liability payments (only with supporting text, so a real expense is never hidden). Essential vs discretionary by keyword groups (EN/FR/Arabic transliteration) with per-merchant overrides; fallback manual expenses + essential share when there is no transaction data.
- **Emergency fund (step 3)**: target = M months (3 to 6) x (essential expenses + planned liabilities); current = balances of the Cash accounts marked emergency fund; while below target, 10-20 % (default 15) of Net Investable Cash is flagged for diversion, capped at the gap; once funded, 100 % is unlocked. The 3 to 6 months range is "commonly cited guidance"; the exact wording of the Banque de France, MoneyHelper, CFPB and UAE sources was NOT verified (see [[Emergency-Fund|Emergency Fund]]). Settings and the emergency-account flag live in localStorage (per device); a profile column would be needed to follow the user across devices.
- UI `components/cash-flow-waterfall.tsx` (Expert-style): bar ladder, 12-month velocity line with scrub readout, expenses and exclusions block, coverage ruler with 3 and 6 month ticks. 74 `cfw_` keys.
- **UAE gratuity** `lib/uae-gratuity.ts` (37 tests): Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021 Art. 51 as checked against public sources (basic wage only, 21 days per year for the first 5 years then 30, pro rata fractions, daily wage = basic / 30, cap 24 months of basic, unpaid leave excluded, LAST basic wage; free zones such as DIFC/ADGM and UAE nationals warned, not computed; `UAE_GRATUITY_RULES.asOf` 2026-10-09). Outputs entitlement at the end date, paid so far, outstanding, since-last-payment view, a period-accrual comparison, and the difference against the employer's stated figure. Storage: **migration `0040_end_of_service_plans.sql` DRAFTED, NOT APPLIED**; actions `dashboard/gratuity-actions.ts`; component `gratuity-manager.tsx` (the calculator works without the table, Save is disabled). 94 `grat_` keys. Steve's own workbook was used only to check the method in the chat and none of his figures are in the repo.
- **Not verified:** all of this in light theme, RTL and the nine languages; the heuristics on real statements; the gratuity table and actions against a real database.
